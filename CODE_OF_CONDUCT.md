# Code of conduct

Be straightforward and courteous. Critique entries, not people.

Security work attracts strong opinions about vendors, researchers, and whose fault a flaw is.
Keep it out of the database. An entry describes a flaw and what to do about it; it does not
assign blame, and it does not editorialise about the vendor's competence or the researcher's
motives.

Not acceptable: harassment of any kind, personal attacks, publishing others' private
information, or using this project to pressure a vendor or a researcher.

Report a problem to the maintainers at conduct@gpuvulndb.org. Reports are handled privately.
Maintainers may edit, lock, or remove contributions and ban accounts.
